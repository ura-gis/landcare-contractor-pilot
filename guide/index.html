Contractor user guide

1. Submit your first survey

  1. Open the LandCare Contractor Portal on your phone, tablet, or computer. Contractors can use the public app without an ArcGIS account.
  2. Choose your Organization and confirm the Service period.
  3. Open Submit Service. Tap a parcel on the map, or use Add an assigned parcel. You may select more than one parcel.
  4. Review the selected parcel list and the blue boundaries. Remove any parcel that should not be included.
  5. Choose Continue or Continue to service form.
  6. Check the prefilled assignment information, confirm Yes or No, attach one photo, and add any comments.
  7. Choose Submit survey. Keep the page open until you see the success confirmation.
  8. Review the confirmation and your updated progress. Choose Survey more parcels to continue.

Before you start: Have an internet connection and a photo of the completed work or current parcel conditions. Do not rely on the app for offline submissions.

2. Choose the right service period

LandCare service periods run from the 15th through the 14th of the following month, Eastern time. The period label uses the month in which the period begins.

Service period example
Period labelDates covered
2026-10October 15 through November 14, 2026
2026-09September 15 through October 14, 2026

Submit immediately after completing work, preferably the same day, and before the period ends. Expand Dates and survey submission rules in the app to check the dates.

Current is the normal choice. Use Previous when submitting a late survey for work belonging to the previous service period. Confirm the selected period before continuing: it determines which assignment period receives the survey.

The app records when you submit. It does not ask for a separate date of service. A late survey keeps its actual submission timestamp even when credited to the previous period.

3. Find and select parcels

Map and parcel labels

Labels show the short block & lot, such as 27-A-129. Zoom in to see labels. Some labels are hidden when parcels are too close together; zoom closer or select the parcel. The full parcel number is retained for matching surveys to assignments.

Use the + and − controls to zoom. Drag to move around the map. On a phone, use familiar pinch and drag gestures. Check the boundary and nearby streets before selecting a parcel.

Street map and aerial imagery

Choose 2025 imagery to view EagleView aerial photography. Choose Street map to switch back. Imagery helps identify the place, but it is a 2025 photograph and may not reflect current conditions. Parcel outlines and your selection remain visible over either basemap.

Find my location

Choose Find my location and allow location access if prompted. The map centers on your device’s estimated position. Accuracy depends on the device and conditions; confirm the actual parcel boundary yourself. Location does not select parcels or submit a survey for you.

Select one parcel or a group

On Submit Service, tap parcels or add them from the parcel dropdown. Selected parcels have blue outlines and appear in the selected parcel list. Use the removal control beside a parcel to leave it out, or Clear selection to start over. Review the count before opening the survey.

One answer and one photo cover every parcel in a group. Group parcels only when the same Yes/No answer and photo accurately represent them. Use separate surveys for different outcomes. If a parcel needs separate documentation, leave it out of the group and submit a separate survey for that parcel.

Changing search or filters can hide parcels already selected. Check the selected parcel list and any hidden-selection warning; hiding a parcel does not automatically remove it from your survey.

Active and Request Only

Active parcels are regular monthly assignments. Request Only parcels have dashed boundaries. Submit surveys for Request Only parcels only after the LandCare team asks you to service them. The app allows these surveys, but it does not confirm that a request was issued. Request Only surveys are counted separately from Active monthly completion.

4. Complete the survey

FieldWhat to do
Assignment informationCheck the parcel selection, organization, and assignment period. These values come from the app. Use Change selected parcels if the selection is wrong.
Contract obligations: Yes / NoYes is selected by default. Confirm Yes only if you fulfilled the contractual obligations for every selected parcel. Choose No if you did not. Separate parcels with different outcomes into separate surveys.
Photo of completed workRequired for both Yes and No. For Yes, photograph the completed work. For No, show current conditions. Attach one clear photo that represents the selected parcels.
CommentsOptional. Note anything the LandCare team should know, such as construction, dumping, access issues, property damage, or other conditions needing attention. For a No answer, explain what prevented completion.

After choosing Submit survey, wait for confirmation. Do not close the page while it says it is submitting. An uploaded photo or filled form alone does not mean the survey has been submitted.

If you change parcels or periods after opening a survey, the app may warn that your answer, photo, and comments will be cleared. Keep the survey open to finish it, or discard it deliberately and start with the correct selection.

The app can restore saved answer and comments in the same tab after an interruption. Photos must be attached again. Always review restored information before submitting.

5. Understand statuses and progress

Open Completion Overview to review assignments in Map view or List view. Select a parcel to see its survey history, comments, and available photo evidence.

Status or map styleMeaning
Green · Survey complete (Yes)The latest valid survey for this parcel and period has a Yes answer.
Orange · Needs follow-upThe latest valid survey has a No answer. The parcel does not count as a completed Yes.
Gray · No surveyNo matching valid survey has been found for this parcel in the selected period. This does not prove whether work occurred.
Dashed purple · Request OnlyThe assignment is serviced when requested. This is an assignment type, separate from survey status. A Request Only parcel can also have a Yes or No survey.
Blue outline · Selected parcelThe parcel is selected or focused. Blue is not a completion status.
Status unavailableSurvey results could not be loaded. Do not treat this as No survey or assume the parcel needs another submission.

Expand the organization progress summary to see Active assignment totals. Active remaining means Active parcels without a latest Yes: those with No surveys plus those with no survey. Request Only parcels are excluded from that monthly denominator.

The map legend counts describe the parcels currently shown. Organization progress totals describe the whole selected organization and period, so they can differ from filtered map counts. Request Only is a separate type and may overlap a survey-status count; do not add it to those counts.

A contractor’s Yes answer is not staff approval or inspection. The pilot shows contractor-submitted information.

6. Use search and filters

Find a parcel searches block & lot, parcel number, or address. Clear the search text to see other parcels again.

Expand Filter parcels to choose Active + Request Only, Active only, or Request Only, and a survey status. Search, assignment type, and status work together. For example, Active only + No survey shows Active assignments without a matching survey for the chosen period.

Active remaining shows Active parcels without a latest Yes; it is unavailable when Request Only is selected. Use Clear filters to reset search and parcel filters. Check the “Showing …” summary to understand the current scope.

In the overview list, select a column heading to sort. Select it again to reverse the order. Use View surveys / submit survey to review a parcel.

7. Correct or repeat a survey

If a selected parcel already has a survey for the same period, the app warns you before opening another survey. Review the existing entry first. Continue only if another visit or correction is needed.

The most recent valid submission controls the parcel’s displayed status for that period. A newer No replaces an earlier Yes in progress counts; a newer Yes replaces an earlier No. Older entries remain in the history. A group survey creates one linked parcel entry for each selected parcel.

If you accidentally chose the wrong organization, period, or parcel, contact the LandCare team with the submission details. Do not submit another survey for an unrelated parcel to try to erase the first one.

8. Troubleshooting and getting help

I cannot find a parcel.
Confirm organization and service period, clear search and filters, and try the block & lot or address. If it is still missing, ask the LandCare team to check the assignment.
My location does not appear.
Allow location in your browser and device settings, then try Find my location again. Use the map, imagery, address, and parcel list if location is unavailable.
Imagery will not load.
The app restores the street map if imagery fails. You can continue on the street map or try imagery again after checking your connection.
Progress is unavailable or taking too long.
Check your connection and use the refresh control under Troubleshooting and survey totals. Unavailable results do not mean no surveys exist. Avoid submitting duplicates just to change a count.
The form says its connection needs attention or parcel count is incorrect.
Do not submit that form. Return to parcel selection and reopen it. If it persists, send the LandCare team the selected parcels and a screenshot of the error.
Submission failed, timed out, or I am unsure it went through.
Check the parcel’s survey history and refresh results before retrying. If you see the new entry, do not submit it again. If unsure, contact the LandCare team.
I refreshed and my photo is gone.
Reattach it. Restored drafts include answer and comments, not the photo.
The app on my phone looks different.
Controls stack vertically on small screens. Scroll to the selected parcel list and survey section; the same workflow applies. A localhost or 127.0.0.1 link only works on the computer running the test server. Share the public website link instead.

For help, contact your usual LandCare team contact. Include your organization, service period, block & lot or parcel number, approximate submission time, and a screenshot of the problem. Never send passwords or account credentials.
